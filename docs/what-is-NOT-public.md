# What Is NOT Public (`docs/what-is-NOT-public.md`)

To balance open-source educational value with intellectual property protection, the following assets are **intentionally excluded** from this public repository:

1. **Physical Test Stand Experimental Datasets:**
   Raw and processed 30-day sensor logs, empirical $h_{\text{eff}}(t)$ curves, and laboratory validation reports remain private commercial assets.

2. **Optimized Anti-Scaling & Brine Management Regimes:**
   The exact cross-flow velocity profiles, proprietary surface treatments, brine blowdown hydraulics, and experimentally optimized chemical dosing schedules are confidential know-how. The values in `CONFIG` inside `simulator.html` are generic illustrative placeholders.

3. **Full Mechanical & Dry-Cooler Engineering Passport:**
   Detailed CAD drawings, flange sealing specifications, internal baffle geometries, and the full system engineering passport are restricted.

4. **Trademarks & Brand Assets:**
   Per Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0 and the `NOTICE` file, the "SolarDes" name and associated logos are protected trademarks.
